# Product Brief

## Problem
Cross-border sellers face fragmented product education across awareness, readiness, onboarding, and launch. Product marketing teams also need a way to keep AI-generated seller education consistent with approved value propositions and boundaries.

## Product concept
GlobalLaunch Studio turns product messaging into structured agent skills and routes sellers through a unified educational journey. It provides one messaging source of truth, a lightweight product knowledge layer, seller simulation, content generation, and measurable evaluation.

## Primary users
- Product Marketing Manager
- Seller Education / Enablement team
- Sales / Account team
- Cross-border seller operations team
