# Portfolio Copy

## Project title
GlobalLaunch Studio — AI-powered seller education and GTM orchestration

## Short description
Designed and developed a functional product marketing platform that converts cross-border selling narratives into reusable AI agent skills, bilingual seller education, and a stage-based onboarding journey.

## Resume bullets
- Designed a five-stage seller education architecture spanning awareness, consideration, readiness, onboarding, and activation, defining which AI agent delivers which knowledge at each stage.
- Structured six bilingual agent skills with product-marketing objectives, seller inputs, required messaging, output expectations, and claim guardrails.
- Built a working seller simulator that routes Mandarin and English questions, personalizes education for four cross-border seller personas, and evaluates CTA, grounding, context, and prohibited claims.
- Created a governed messaging library and content generator for seller emails, FAQs, and launch checklists without relying on fabricated business metrics or internal company data.

## Interview framing
I did not want to build another generic chatbot. I started from the product marketing workflow: a product team has approved narratives, different seller segments have different questions, and sellers need different knowledge at different stages. I therefore treated prompts as governed product assets and designed a reference architecture connecting seller context, journey stage, agent skill, approved messaging, next action, and quality evaluation.
